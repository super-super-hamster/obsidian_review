## 一、总览

**功能句 = 可以提前背好、写任何题型都能直接套用的句子模块。**

### 贯穿全课的写作方法论

1. **适用面**：这些句子「小作文能用，大作文也能用」，书信、通知、图画、图表、文字材料（读后感）乃至会议纪要都能套。
2. **同义替换是防重复的关键**：同一句话里每个实词都给出 3–6 个替换，考生不必担心与别人撞句（如「非常」= quite / very / particularly / exceedingly / extraordinary / so / pretty；「明显」= obvious / remarkable / striking / sharp / evident / apparent）。
3. **不确定词性时降级处理**：拿不准第三人称单数，就在动词前加 can / will / may；不认识高级词就用简单词顶替（manufacturers → makers / producers / companies；approximate 不会写就写 about / roughly）。
4. **语法手段优先于生词**：定语从句可换成 `-ing` 形式（which clearly illustrates → clearly illustrating）；`-ing` 表伴随状语；强调句 `It is ... that ...`；全倒装（介词短语＋谓语＋主语）、半倒装（否定副词置于句首）。
5. **先搭框架再填内容**：老师主张「给任何题目都先写一句套话开场」，例如图画作文一律先写「这是一幅简单但极具启发性的漫画，它清晰展示了……」，然后再接题干信息。
6. **反复强调「动笔写」**：写作核心是动笔，不是背；听课后要看着汉语自己复写一遍，再跟真题逐句练。
7. **命题趋势提醒**：考研英语作文「旧瓶装新酒」，考过的话题改一改还会再考，不要因为考过就放弃准备。

## 二、逐篇要点

### 01｜功能句 1–5：自我介绍、寒暄、写信目的、提建议、展望未来

本篇无句数以外的主题，是课程开篇。老师先做自我介绍（课件中姓名前后写作「沈鹏」「石雷鹏」「石雪峰」，属转写不一致），介绍教学经历（2008 年起讲授四六级与考研写作）与写作课理念。

| 编号 | 功能 | 英文模板 | 可替换点 / 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 自我介绍（as + 身份 + 定语从句） | `As a senior at this university who is fond of reading in the library in spare time, I am writing for the purpose of ...` | 身份随收信对象变化（大学生／读者／顾客）；介词 at / of / from / in 均可；「喜欢」= be fond of / be keen on / prefer / love / like |
| 2 | 寒暄（高兴回想起过去某段时间） | `I am quite pleased to recall the past two months, when I worked as an editor for your magazine.` | 「非常」与「高兴」(pleased / happy / glad / delighted / excited) 均可替换；when 后接**过去时**，主句用一般现在时 |
| 2′ | 寒暄变体（班级活动） | `I am quite pleased to recall the past year, when our class held the first charity sale.` | 也可用被动：The first charity sale was held by our class. |
| 3 | 写信目的 | `I am writing for the purpose of making suggestions to enhance the service here.` / 简写 `... to do sth.` | 可套通知（This notice is for the purpose of recruiting 25 volunteers）、图画（This drawing is for the purpose of illustrating ...）、图表（This chart is ...）、会议（This meeting is for the purpose of discussing ...） |
| 4 | 提建议（替代 you should） | `It is advisable for you to ...` / `You are supposed to ...` / `You are well advised to ...` / `It is exceedingly necessary for you to ...` / `It is of great necessity for sb. to ...` | 例句：`I deem it is advisable for you to apply for the position of English teaching in a training organization.`；`You are supposed to prolong the opening hours of the reading rooms, where students study.` |
| 4′ | 递进衔接词 | in addition / additionally / besides / what is more / moreover / furthermore | 例句：`In addition, it is exceedingly necessary for parents to provide their children with opportunities and freedom to grow independently.`（独立 = on their own / independently / by themselves） |
| 4″ | 因果衔接词 | thus / hence / therefore / so / accordingly / as a result / as a consequence | 例句：`Thus, Chinese car manufacturers are supposed to pay more attention to the domestic market, owing to its huge potential.`（国内市场 = the domestic market / the market at home；因为＋名词用 owing to / because of，＋句子用 because / since / as） |
| 4‴ | 有必要做某事 | `It is of great necessity for sb. to ...` | 例句：`... to formulate comprehensive strategies to enhance the quality of cars and after-sales service.` |
| 5 | 展望未来 | `If they do their utmost to do sth., the future of the mobile phone industry will be promising / hopeful / brilliant.` | 「全力以赴」= do one's utmost / try one's best / spare no efforts |

### 02｜功能句 6–10：感谢、不满、期待、值得、原因、强调

| 编号 | 功能 | 英文模板 | 可替换点 / 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | 表达感谢 | `I am deeply grateful to you for your hard work.` | be grateful / indebted / obliged to sb. for sth.；记不住就写 thankful 或 thank you very much |
| 6′ | 表达不满 | `I am deeply disappointed with the quality of your product(s).` | be disappointed with / in；降级写法 very angry / outrageous / quite upset about |
| 7 | 期待回复 / 期待参加 | `I am looking forward to your favorable reply at your earliest convenience.`<br>`I sincerely hope that you can participate in this contest.` | to 是介词，后接名词短语；衷心期待 = I am sincerely looking forward to your participation in this contest；参加 = participate in / take part in / join |
| 8 | 某事值得做 | `This movie is particularly worth watching for two reasons.` | be worth + 名词/doing；可换 this book / this site / these two cartoons are particularly worth attention |
| 9 | 引出原因 | `What resulted in the differences above? To my understanding, at least two reasons can account for this phenomenon.` | 加不加 -ed 取决于题干时间；在我看来 = to my understanding / from my perspective；解释 = account for / explain；现象 = phenomenon / trend |
| 10 | 强调句（论述原因或重要性） | `It is your devotion to work that enables us to enjoy a positive atmosphere of learning here.` | It is ... that ... 去掉后句子仍完整；否定版：It is their pessimistic attitude that calls them not to see any hope / chance / possibility of change |

> 本篇另附：第八句可作「推荐电影／书／景点」的固定开头；第九、十句常用于分析原因段落。

### 03｜功能句 11–15：现象与原因、突出特征、对比、排比、活动信息

| 编号 | 功能 | 英文模板 | 可替换点 / 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 11 | 现象值得关注＋原因 | `This trend is particularly worth attention, for the reason that they focus only on the virtual world, ignoring the real life.` | 主语可换 this trend / phenomenon / fact；忽略 = ignoring / overlooking / neglecting；只关注 = focus only on / pay attention just to / concentrate only on |
| 12 | 描述突出特征 | `College life is characterized by a rich variety of campus activities.`<br>`... is characterized by the fact that there are ...` | be characterized by / be marked by / be featured with；丰富多样 = a rich variety of / various / all kinds of / diverse；成功人士 = successful people / individuals / figures / celebrities / social elites / high achievers；优秀品质 = diligence, optimism, confidence, perseverance, a strong will |
| 12′ | 描述国家（现在完成时） | `In recent years, developing countries have been characterized by their economic boom and prosperity, which has enabled local residents to possess additional money to buy mobile phones.` | 繁荣 = boom / prosperity；当地居民 = local residents / people / citizens；拥有 = have / possess / own / gain / enjoy；钱 = money / funds / incomes / salaries |
| 13 | 与……相比 | `Compared with students' demands in learning, the number and variety of books here are not adequate.` | compared with / compared to；数量与种类 = the number and variety / diversity / sorts / categories；不足 = not adequate / inadequate / not enough / not sufficient / not ample |
| 13′ | 对比＋结论 | `Compared with the past years, the relations between China and Japan have become worse. So a large proportion of residents in China did not purchase Japanese products, including vehicles.` | 与过去相比可省 years；糟糕比较级是 worse（不要写 badder）；包括 = including |
| 14 | 排比（表达意义/重要性） | `Practice and diligence will bring you experience and inspiration, arouse your passion for learning and help you make progress every day.` | 三个动词顺序可任意调换；我相信 = I am firmly convinced that / from my point of view；持之以恒 = perseverance / persistence / the spirit of never giving up；克服 = overcome / conquer |
| 14′ | 排比套用（留学/考研） | `Studying abroad can give youngsters more opportunities, inspire their enthusiasm for foreign cultures and help them broaden their horizons.` | can 后用动词原形，避免三单错误 |
| 15 | 活动背景信息（通知/书信） | `The ceremony is scheduled to start at nine o'clock on June 26 in the Center of Students Activities, which will last for approximately two hours.` | be scheduled to / be planned / be arranged to / be going to / will；持续 = last for；大约 = approximately / about / roughly；也可用 and 并列（and last for ...） |

### 04｜功能句 16–20：忙碌背景、表明观点、图画概述、图画细节、反差

| 编号 | 功能 | 英文模板 | 可替换点 / 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 16 | 描述忙碌背景（拒绝/解释） | `As you know, during the past six months, I was particularly busy, because I focused all my time and energy on the preparation for the national entrance examinations for postgraduate studies.` | 「非常」多种升级；投入 = focus ... on / devote all time or energy to；考研全称与简称 the postgraduate entrance exams |
| 17 | 表明观点（先身份再观点） | `As a senior who is preparing for the postgraduate entrance exams, I am firmly convinced that perseverance always brings me great power.` | 我认为 = I am convinced / deem / believe / reckon / hold / maintain / claim to the idea that；力量 = power / strength / motivation |
| 17′ | 个人喜好（插入语） | `As a youngster, I prefer to work in cities and live in the countryside.` | 年轻人 = youngster / young person / young man / young guy；城市 = cities / urban areas，农村 = the countryside / rural areas |
| 18 | 概述图画/图表/文字材料 | `This is a simple but enlightening cartoon, which vividly illustrates a common phenomenon among ...` | 启发性 = enlightening / thought-provoking / meaningful / profound / inspiring / illuminating；负面可换 ironic / satirical / absurd / ridiculous；展示 = illustrates / shows / presents / displays；也可用 -ing 形式 vividly illustrating |
| 18′ | 文字材料（读后感） | `This is a simple but inspiring excerpt of an article, in which the author points out that ...` | 节选 excerpt（题干通常给出）；作者 = the author / the writer；指出 = points out / argues / maintains / contends / reckons |
| 19 | 图画细节描写 | `On the path to the peak of a high mountain, there is a youngster sitting for a rest and sighing, "I'm too tired to climb."`<br>倒装：`Beside a table sit four young people, staring at their smartphones, with tasty food uneaten.` | 地点＋there be＋人物＋行为＋伴随动作＋文字；全倒装 = 介词短语＋谓语＋主语；伴随 = -ing，被动/完成用 -ed（uneaten）；with 复合结构（with a sweet smile on his face） |
| 20 | 反差对比 | `Although they gather to enhance friendship, these guys scarcely chat or communicate with each other.`<br>`Although I have no children, I cannot agree more with the author's idea.` | 尽管用 although，后面不再加 but；几乎不 = scarcely，置于句首构成半倒装（Scarcely do these guys communicate with each other）；完全同意 = I cannot agree more |

### 05｜功能句 21–25：文字说明、图表概述、数据变化、排序对比、联系现实

| 编号 | 功能 | 英文模板 | 可替换点 / 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 21 | 描述图画下方文字说明 | `We can notice two Chinese characters below the picture, which read "..." on the way.` | 也可被动 can be noticed / can be seen；一些汉字 = some / certain / a number of / a few Chinese characters；内容 = which read / which say；位置可放段首或段尾 |
| 22 | 图表概述 | `This is a column chart, which clearly illustrates the dramatic changes in the market shares of three car brands in China from 2008 to 2009.` | column chart / bar chart；也可 there is / it is / provided / presented / given is；明显 = dramatic / obvious / remarkable / striking / sharp；数据两类：同类数据的变化、不同数据的排序与对比 |
| 23 | 数据上升 | `During this period, the share of domestic brands ascended remarkably, reaching approximately 33%.` | 上升 = ascend / increase / rise / climb / go up；明显（副词）= remarkably / obviously / strikingly / sharply / quickly / evidently / apparently；达到用 -ing（reaching / accounting for） |
| 24 | 数据下降 / 不变 | `By contrast, the sales of Japanese brands showed a striking decline, from 35% to 26%.`<br>`Meanwhile, the proportion of American brands remained stable at 12%.` | 下降 = decline / drop / fall / decrease / a downward trend；不变 = remain / stay stable / unchanged；也可用 there be 句式（there was a striking rise / no change） |
| 24′ | 排序与对比 | `Overall, seniors have the largest proportion of part-time job takers, at 88.24%. The next are juniors and sophomores, at 71.93% and 71.13% respectively. Finally come freshmen, at 67.77%.` | 总体上 = overall；最高比例 = the largest proportion / percentage；其次 = the next / then；分别 = respectively；全倒装 Finally come freshmen；排名 = which ranks first / second |
| 24″ | 内部占比（饼图式） | `Overall, the expense on new year gifts is the largest proportion, accounting for 40%. The next is the expenditure on transportation and dining, both at 20%.` | 花销 = expense / expenditure / spending；交通 = transportation（不是 traffic）；就餐 = dining；其他项目 = other items |
| 25 | 图表/图画与现实联系 | `This drawing is apparently associated with two distinct attitudes in front of adversities.`<br>`Such statistics can definitely be associated with the fierce competition in China's car market.` | 显然 = apparently / obviously / evidently / definitely / undoubtedly / with no doubt；联系 = be associated with / linked to / related to；截然不同 = distinct / different / totally different；逆境 = adversities / hardships / setbacks / challenges / difficulties / troubles；激烈 = fierce / intense |

### 06｜功能句 26–30：现象影射、危害、比喻、号召、举例

| 编号 | 功能 | 英文模板 | 可替换点 / 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 26 | 现象影射现实（转折） | `However, in reality, a host of youngsters tend to abandon when they encounter setbacks during their pursuit of success.` | 然而 = however / nonetheless；很多 = a host of / a large number of / a lot of / a great many；年轻人 = youngsters / young people / young men / teenagers；放弃 = abandon / give up / stop；遭遇 = encounter / meet / have，也可用 when encountering |
| 27 | 危害/障碍 | `As a matter of fact, the thought of giving up may constitute a barrier, which hinders many people's growth and progress in work, life and learning.` | 事实上 = actually / in fact / as a matter of fact；构成 = constitute / form / become；障碍 = barrier / obstacle；阻碍 = hinder / hamper / harm / undermine；也可用 -ing 后置定语 hindering |
| 28 | 比喻 | `To my mind, persistence can be likened to a road leading to success.`<br>`Confidence can be seen as a key, unlocking the gate to a promising future.` | 在我看来 = to my mind / to my understanding / from my point of view / as far as I am concerned / in my opinion；比喻 = be likened to / be seen as / is just like；主动版：We can liken persistence to a road leading to success |
| 29 | 发出号召 | `Thus, everyone is supposed to bear in mind that never give up and you will eventually achieve your dream.`<br>`Society as a whole should foster a positive atmosphere to promote and encourage public virtues.` | 牢牢记住 = bear in mind / keep in mind / remember；应该 = should / be supposed to / it is advisable for sb. to；营造 = foster / create；氛围 = atmosphere / environment；正面用 promote / encourage，负面用 reject / refuse / criticize / say no to |
| 30 | 举例与列举 | `I am a case in point.` / `A typical example is myself.`<br>`We should utilize modern technological products in a rational way, including the Internet, mobile phones, TV, computers and AI.` | 除了 for example：a case in point / a typical example；举例内容继续套用功能句（如排比句）；列举 = including / such as（后接名词或 -ing） |
