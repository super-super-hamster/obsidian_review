# 考研英语一大小作文通用模板与高频场景语料库

这份文档将考研英语一大小作文的**可通用部分最大化提炼**：小作文通过**“1套书信终极骨架 + 3大主题替换模块 + 2套特殊格式外壳（通知/纪要）”**解决所有17种细分应用文；大作文通过**“4类题型首段专属描写 + 2套跨题型二三段通用母模板”**覆盖图画、动态图表、静态图表、混合与文字全题型，并附带**校园活动、中国传统文化、历史名家、图画动作与图表数据**等全套主题句与描述语料。

---

## 第一部分：小作文（Part A）最大公约数通用模板与分主题替换

### 一、 小作文核心破题逻辑：如何用最少背诵量通杀17种应用文？

无论考纲怎么变化，小作文的本质都是**“格式外壳 + 三段式任务打卡（约100词）”**：
1. **第一段（1–2句，约20–25词）**：寒暄/自报家门 + 写信/发文目的。
2. **第二段（3–4句，约50–65词）**：用 `To begin with, ... In addition, ... Finally, ...` 分三点逐一回应题干指令（是什么/为什么/怎么办）。
3. **第三段（1–2句，约15–20词）**：重申感谢/建议/期盼 + 联系方式与期待回复。

>

### 三、 书信三大主题模块的精准替换表（直接嵌入上方通用骨架）

将13种书信按底层逻辑归纳为**三大主题模块**，只需替换首段目的短语、中段核心句式与尾段第一句即可无缝切换：

#### 主题模块 1：求知 / 促进行动 / 日常回复类（占历年考频 70% 以上）

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. 建议信**<br>（对个人/朋友） | `share a few of my humble opinions on [主题]`<br>（分享我对……的几点粗浅看法） | • **建议1**：`I strongly advise you to focus on [建议一], as it can...`<br>• **建议2**：`It is essential for you to [建议二].`<br>• **建议3**：`Don't forget to [建议三]; otherwise, you will [不良后果: miss out on ideal opportunities / not have enough time to get ready].` | `It is hoped that my suggestions will be of great help to you.` |

### 四、 通知（Notice）与会议纪要（Minutes）通用模板

通知与会议纪要的正文完全复用了上述书信的 `To begin with / In addition / Finally` 逻辑，唯一区别在于**首尾格式外壳**与**会议纪要的时态红线**。

#### 1. 通知/告示（Notice）全场景通用模板（比赛活动 · 招募志愿者 · 设施服务介绍）

```text
                                 Notice
                                                       December 21, 2026
    Aiming to [发文目的：enrich our campus culture / guarantee the smooth operation of Prof. Smith's research project / help newly enrolled international students get better acquainted with our library], [主办方：the Students' Union / our research team] is pleased to announce that [活动/招募/设施名称] will officially commence / be open at [地点：the school auditorium / the campus center] on/from [时间：next Friday]. The relevant details are as follows.
    To begin with, the upcoming [event / position / facility] features [亮点或主要职责：a variety of interactive sessions / collecting survey data on campus sports / a vast collection of academic resources], which will provide a marvelous opportunity to [益处：display your talents / satisfy your diverse study needs]. In addition, [participants / ideal applicants] are expected to [具体要求：submit a list of works in advance / possess a good command of English and a strong sense of responsibility]. Finally, please note that [补充福利或规定：rich prizes await the top three winners / you should have at least three days of availability during this period / the facility opens from 8:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. on weekdays].
    Those who are interested are warmly welcome to sign up / reach out via [邮箱：union@university.edu.cn] no later than [截止日期：next Wednesday]. We eagerly await your active involvement!
                                         [落款：The Students' Union / Li Ming]
```

## 第二部分：大作文（Part B）跨题型通用模板（4大题型 · 首段切换 + 二三段通用）

### 一、 大作文通用化核心秘诀：为什么二、三段可以90%通用？

考研英语一大作文虽然在第一段呈現出**图画、动态图表、静态图表、混合（图画+图表/文字）、纯文字**五种外观，但只要在第一段最后一句话将题目统一收束为**“这反映了现代社会对【核心主题词】的日益重视 / 揭示了【某种趋势】”**，到了第二段和第三段，就只剩下两种固定写法：
- **通用母版 A（价值与意义论述型）**：适用于所有**图画类、文字类、以及偏重倡导某品质/建设的混合类**（核心写：为什么这个品质/现象重要？对个人心智、能力竞争、社会文化有什么积极意义或反面危害？）。
- **通用母版 B（趋势与成因剖析型）**：适用于所有**动态图表、静态图表、以及偏重解释数据变化的混合类**（核心写：为什么会出现这一数据趋势或比例分布？从经济民生、科技行业发展、个人核心诉求三个维度剖析原因）。

### 二、 第一段（Paragraph 1）：五大题型专属引入与描述模板（约45–60词）

考场上根据试卷给出的材料形式，直接从以下 5 个首段模块中五选一：

#### 1. 【图画类·单图动态首段】（适用2023龙舟赛、2021学唱戏、2019爬山、2018选课、2015聚餐玩手机）
>     **Depicted in the portrayal is a common yet thought-provoking scene in which** `[画中主体：two teams of dragon boat competitors / a confused boy / four young people]` **is/are** `[主要动作：rowing on the river energetically / selecting courses in front of a computer / staring at their smartphones intently]`, **with** `[伴随神态或环境：numerous spectators cheering them on / a look of hesitation on his face / delicious dishes ignored on the table]`. *(若有说话内容)* **Meanwhile, one person remarks cheerfully/wearily, "**`[人物对话翻译]`**".** *(若有图下小标题)* **Underneath the cartoon, a brief caption reads: "**`[小标题翻译]`**".** **Simple as the drawing seems at first glance, it subtly aims to emphasize the supreme importance of** `[核心主题词：inheriting traditional culture / courage to face challenges / rational smartphone use]`.

#### 2. 【图画类·双图或态度对比首段】（适用2022听讲座、2020做事习惯、2017有书与读书、2016父母榜样、2014母女相携）
>     **Depicted in the portrayal are two entirely distinct** `[attitudes towards / states of]` `[对比对象：a campus lecture / handling tasks / family education]`. **In the first scenario,** `[主体A：a girl / a young man]` **is** `[动作A：studying hard to finish tasks early / complaining that a cross-disciplinary lecture is useless]`. **By stark contrast,** `[主体B：the other individual]` **chooses to** `[动作B：delay until the last minute on the sofa / respond cheerfully that attending it must be beneficial]`. *(若有小标题)* **Underneath the cartoon, a brief caption reads: "**`[小标题翻译]`**".** **The thought-provoking portrayal essentially aims to underscore the immense value of** `[核心主题词：proper time management / extensive learning / role modeling]`.

#### 3. 【图表类·动态图首段】（有时间推移，如2025年真题2014–2023居民耐用消费品表）
>     **The provided** `[table / line graph / bar chart]` **vividly illustrates the changing pattern in** `[图表主题：the ownership of major durable consumer goods per 100 households in China]` **between** `[起始年份：2014]` **and** `[终止年份：2023]`. **Based upon the data, the figure for** `[主对象A：air conditioners]` **surged significantly from** `[起点数据：75.2]` **to** `[终点数据：145.9]`, **peaking at the end of the period.** *(若B同升)* **Similarly,** `[次对象B：washing machines and refrigerators]` **experienced a parallel upward trend, climbing steadily to** `[终点数据：98.2 and 103.4 respectively]`. *(若B下降则换为：By contrast, 项B witnessed an opposing trend, dropping from X to Y.)* **Behind these changing numbers lies a deeper social trend concerning** `[核心主题词：the improvement of living standards]` **that deserves our attention.**

#### 4. 【图表类·静态图首段】（无时间推移/比例分布，如2026年真题养老机器人接受度饼图+关注点柱状图）
>     **The provided** `[pie chart / bar chart / charts]` **clearly reveal(s) the results of a survey concerning** `[调查主题：consumers' acceptance of and primary concerns regarding elderly care robots]`. **Specifically,** `[占比最高项A：consumers who fully accept them]` **dominate(s) the chart, accounting for the largest proportion at** `[最高比例：39.3%]`, **followed closely by** `[次高项B：partial acceptance at 32.8%]`. **Meanwhile / By contrast,** `[另一维度最高项或最低项对比：regarding their primary concerns, safety ranks first at 46.30%, whereas convenience merely makes up a negligible 10.70%]`. **This distinct distribution is closely linked to the shifting priorities regarding** `[核心主题词：smart eldercare]` **in our society.**

#### 5. 【混合类 & 文字类首段】（2024图画+图表混合 / 名言摘录文字类）
- **混合类（图画+图表/文字）首段**：
  >     **The combination of the portrayal and the** `[bar chart / excerpt]` **vividly illustrates the current situation of** `[共同主题：urban park construction and public fitness]`. **As is vividly depicted in the former,** `[图画主体与动作：several citizens are happily jogging in a newly-built park]`, **with one remarking, "**`[话语翻译]`**". Meanwhile, the latter clearly reveals that** `[图表趋势或文字核心：the number of city parks surged from 406 to 670 between 2020 and 2022]`. **Simple as they seem, these two visuals jointly reflect the growing emphasis on** `[核心主题词]` **in modern society.**
- **纯文字类（名言/观点摘录）首段**：
  >     **Emerging from the provided excerpt is an insightful statement addressing** `[话题]`, **in which the author emphasizes that** `[用自己话转述作者观点]`. **There is no denying that this perspective deserves our profound reflection, especially in the context of** `[当代社会背景]`. **Personally, I firmly endorse this argument, as it aligns closely with the supreme importance of** `[核心主题词]`.

### 三、 第二段 & 第三段（Paragraph 2 & 3）：跨题型终极通用母模板

写完第一段后，直接根据题目属于**“品质/现象/文化论述（母版A）”**还是**“图表数据成因剖析（母版B）”**二选一套入：

#### 通用母版 A：品质 / 文化 / 社会现象论述型（通杀：所有图画类 + 文字类 + 混合类）

> **【Paragraph 2：主题句 + 个人/社会/长远三层递进论证（约85词）】**

### 三、 【大作文首段】图画人物动作与图表数据描写速查表

#### 1. 图画作文：人物身份 + 动作 + 神态 + 场景倒装词块

- **具象动态动词**：
  - 专注投入：`be completely absorbed/immersed in...` (全神贯注于……), `devote oneself to a sea of books/tasks` (全身心投入书海/任务中).
  - 犹豫与挣扎：`stand at a crossroads` (站在十字路口), `face a dilemma between A and B` (面临A与B的两难抉择), `stare at the screen intently` (目不转睛盯着屏幕).
- **神态与情绪介词短语**：
  - 积极神态：`with a bright/confident smile on his face` (面带灿烂/自信的微笑), `cheerfully / encouragingly` (愉快地/鼓励地).
  - 消极神态：`with a puzzled/miserable expression` (面带困惑/痛苦的表情), `in dismay/frustration` (沮丧/挫败地), `with sheer indifference` (极其冷漠地).
- **场景地点倒装与独立主格（首段提分利器）**：
  - `In front of the notice board stand two girls...`（公告栏前站着两名女生……）
  - `Around the dinner table sit four young people...`（餐桌旁坐着四名年轻人……）
  - `At the table sits a confused boy...`（书桌前坐着一个困惑的男孩……）

#### 2. 图表作文：动态趋势、静态占比与五大宏观背景状语
- **动态上升**：`surge / soar / climb / increase + significantly / dramatically / steadily + from A to B, peaking at C in [年份].`

- **五大万能原因状语（直接放在第二段分论点句首）**：
  1. `With the rapid progress in economy and the improvement of living standards, ...`（随着经济的快速发展与生活水平的提高……）

  4. `Under the background of urbanization and modernization, ...`（在城市化与现代化的背景下……）
  5. `Thanks to a series of favorable policies implemented by relevant authorities, ...`（得益于有关部门实施的一系列有利政策……）
