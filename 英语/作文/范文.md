# 1 2005
**题目**：
Two months ago you got a job as an editor for the magazine Designs & Fashions. But now you find that the work is not what you expected. You decide to quit. Write a letter to your boss, Mr. Wang, telling him your decision, stating your reason(s), and making an apology.

**范文**：
Dear Mr. Wang,
It is with deep regret that I am writing to formally resign from my position as an editor at Designs & Fashions. Having worked here for the past two months, I find that this is not an expected job for me.
Specifically, I have come to realize that my true passion lies in creative fashion design, rather than editing text. After thoughtful reflection, I have resolved to pursue a career as an independent designer. I wish to convey my sincere gratitude to you for offering me this valuable opportunity and your guidance. Please accept my sincere apologies for my sudden departure.
To minimize any potential disruption, I am fully committed to assisting in guiding my successor. I would be greatful if you could accept my request at your earliest convenience.
Yours sincerely,
			Li Ming

# 2 2006
**题目**：
You want to contribute to Project Hope by offering financial aid to a child in a remote area. Write a letter to the department concerned, asking them to help find a candidate. You should specify what kind of child you want to help and how you will carry out your plan.

**范文**：
Dear Sir or Madam,
I am writing this letter to express my desire to support Project Hope by offering financial aid to a child who lack food and educational resources and I would be grateful if you could help me find a child who deserves financial support.
Specifically, I would like to support a school-age child who eager to learn but restrained by financial hardship. I can afford his or her daily living expenses and tuition, as well as send some clothes and books on a regular basis. After the child graduates from senior high school, I can continue to support if he or she pursues further education on campus, or I can provide a room to live in if the child works in my city.
Your assistance in indentifing a suitable candidate at your earliest convenience would be greatly appreciated. I am looking forward to receiving your response.
Yours sincerely,
			Li Ming

# 3 2007
**题目**：
Write a letter to your university library, making suggestions for improving its service.

**范文**：
Dear Librarian,
I am writing this letter to make suggestions regarding how to improve our library's services. I would be grateful if you could take my suggestions into consideration.
Specifically, many students fail to return borrowed books within the time limit, mainly because they do not remember the exact expiration date and even forget that they have borrowed books. In my view, setting up an automated email system to remind the students that the deadline is approaching is a simple yet effective way to reduce overdue returns. Additionally, I suggest setting up a dedicated study area for students who are preparing for exams, as many are currently searching for a quiet enviroment to study.
I would truly appreciate it if you could consider these suggestions to make our library a better place.
Yours sincerely,
			Li Ming

# 4 2008
**题目**：
You have just come back from Canada and found a music CD in your luggage that you forgot to return to Bob, your landlord there. Write him a letter to
1) make an apology, and
2) suggest a solution.

**范文**：
Dear Bob,
I am writing this letter to express my sincere apology for forgetting to return your music CD. Upon unpacking my luggage after arriving in China, I found it.   To make amends for my negligence, I would like to propose a few options.
Specifically, I can mail the CD directly to your address via express delivery at my expense, which may take a few days. Alternatively, if it is not in urgent need, I can return it to you in person when I go back to Canada in six months. Additionally, if you plan to visit China, I would be honored to show you around my hometown.
I would be deeply grateful if you could accept my apology. I am looking forward to receiving your response at your earliest convenience.
Yours sincerely,
		Li Ming

        Li Ming
